Agricultural Company v0.3.0

[Production Lines]
- Added a functional Production tab.
- Added timed production jobs that advance every 10 in-game minutes.
- Added persistent production queue and finished-goods inventory.
- Company level now controls simultaneous line slots: 1 / 2 / 3.
- Starting production consumes raw warehouse stock immediately.
- Output quality inherits the lowest quality among consumed ingredients.
- Completed production grants company experience.

[Initial recipes]
- Tomato Juice: Tomato x3 -> x2, 60 minutes (standalone vanilla test line)
- Watermelon Juice: Watermelon-family x2 -> x2, 80 minutes
- Chamoe Gift Set: Korean-melon-family x5 -> x1, 100 minutes, Lv.2
- Salted Napa Cabbage: Napa-cabbage-family x4 -> x2, 120 minutes, Lv.2

[Compatibility]
- Crop Genetics remains optional and independent.
- v0.1/v0.2 save data migrates automatically through default production fields.
- Finished goods are company inventory in 0.3 and are intended to feed the 0.4 contract system.
