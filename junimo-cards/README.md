# Junimo Cards

Stardew Valley collectible card-shop mod.

## v0.3.1 function-hardening loop

- Press `F9` to open the card shop.
- Buy 1 or 5 `Pelican Origins` packs with player gold.
- Each pack contains 5 cards and every card can be clicked individually to reveal it.
- The fifth card is guaranteed Uncommon or better.
- If 9 consecutive packs contain no Rare-or-better card, the next pack's fifth slot guarantees Rare or better.
- Variants: Normal / Holo / Gold / Rainbow.
- Conditions: Good / Near Mint / Mint.
- Collection tracks 30 base cards separately from variant/condition copies.
- Filters: All / Common / Uncommon / Rare / Epic / Legendary / Secret.
- Collection milestone rewards are available at 5 / 10 / 20 / 30 unique cards.
- Extra copies can be placed into 8 persistent sale slots.
- One physical copy of every collected base card is always protected from sale.
- Asking prices can be adjusted in 50G steps within the safe market range.
- At the start of each day, customers make up to 3 purchase attempts.
- Rarity, variant, condition, and asking price affect customer appeal and conversion.
- Daily visitors, sold cards, daily revenue, lifetime revenue, and recent sales history are saved.
- Existing 0.1/0.2/0.3.0 save data is migrated without deleting collection cards.

## First set

`Pelican Origins` contains 30 Stardew-inspired cards ranging from Common to Secret.

## Current development order

v0.3.1 intentionally freezes and hardens the full gameplay functionality first. The final card-shop UI/layout/art pass is the next development stage after runtime testing confirms the gameplay loop.
