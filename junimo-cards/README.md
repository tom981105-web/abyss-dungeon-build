# Junimo Cards

Stardew Valley collectible card-shop mod.

## v0.1.0 core loop

- Press `F8` to open the card shop.
- Buy 1 or 5 `Pelican Origins` packs with player gold.
- Each pack contains 5 cards.
- The fifth card is guaranteed Uncommon or better.
- If 9 consecutive packs contain no Rare-or-better card, the next pack's fifth slot guarantees Rare or better.
- Variants: Normal / Holo / Gold / Rainbow.
- Conditions: Good / Near Mint / Mint.
- Collection is saved per farm save.
- Extra copies can be listed on an 8-slot sale shelf.
- At the start of each day, customers may buy up to 3 listed cards and the sale revenue is paid to the player.

## First set

`Pelican Origins` contains 30 Stardew-inspired cards ranging from Common to Secret.

## Current scope

v0.1.0 intentionally focuses on the addictive loop: pack purchase -> reveal -> collect -> list -> daily sale -> buy more packs. A physical shop map, grading, serial-number cards, NPC collectors, and card battles are future expansion areas, not required for the first playable build.
